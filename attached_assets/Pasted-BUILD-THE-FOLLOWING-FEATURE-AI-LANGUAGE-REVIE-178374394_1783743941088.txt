AI LANGUAGE REVIEW ENGINE
====================================================

Create an enterprise-grade AI Language Review Engine as a core module of the Packaging Compliance Platform.

This module should work alongside FDA, EPA, FTC, USDA, CPSC, eCFR, and Internal Compliance Reviews.
