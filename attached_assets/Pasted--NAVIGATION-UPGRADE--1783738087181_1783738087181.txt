====================================================
SPECIALIST REVIEW EXPERIENCE
====================================================

The application must be optimized for compliance specialists reviewing between 50 and 500 packages per day.

The user should never be forced to manually read an entire package.

The platform should:

Automatically identify violations.

Automatically prioritize critical issues.

Automatically suggest fixes.

Automatically surface relevant regulations.

Automatically show previous resolutions for similar issues.

Everything should be optimized for speed and high-volume review.

====================================================
PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
====================================================

The platform should act like a Senior Packaging Compliance Specialist.

It should:

Find the issue.

Explain the issue.

Show the regulation.

Recommend the fix.

Show historical resolutions.

Provide an approval recommendation.

The user should always leave every review with a clear understanding of:

What failed.

Why it failed.

How to fix it.

What regulation applies.

What should happen next.
