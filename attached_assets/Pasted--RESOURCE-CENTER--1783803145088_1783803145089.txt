RESOURCE CENTER
====================================================

Build a dedicated Resource Center that serves as the central knowledge repository for compliance specialists, managers, legal reviewers, packaging teams, and administrators.

This section should provide transparency into regulatory sources, internal standards, compliance guidance, approved language, and system integrations.

The Resource Center should function as the single source of truth for regulatory and compliance knowledge.

====================================================
SUCCESS CRITERIA
====================================================

The Resource Center should become the single source of truth for the platform.

Users should be able to answer:

- What regulatory sources are connected?
- What endpoint powers this feature?
- What regulations are being checked?
- What validation engine generated this finding?
- What approved language should be used?
- What SOP applies?
- What compliance guidance exists?

The Resource Center should support onboarding, compliance reviews, audits, training, regulatory research, and platform transparency.
