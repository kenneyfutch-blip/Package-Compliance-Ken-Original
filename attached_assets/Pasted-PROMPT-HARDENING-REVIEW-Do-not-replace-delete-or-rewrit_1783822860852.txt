PROMPT HARDENING REVIEW

Do not replace, delete, or rewrite the current Package Review AI prompt.

Instead, perform a comprehensive audit of the existing prompt and determine whether it is sufficiently robust for enterprise-grade packaging compliance analysis.

Your objective is to improve compliance accuracy, reduce hallucinations, increase reliability, and ensure consistent outputs.

Evaluate the existing prompt against the following requirements:

1. ROLE DEFINITION

Verify the AI clearly understands:
- Its purpose
- Its responsibilities
- What it should analyze
- What it should not do

2. HALLUCINATION PROTECTION

Verify the prompt explicitly instructs the AI:

- Never invent regulations
- Never invent compliance requirements
- Never invent legal interpretations
- Never present assumptions as facts
- Never create violations where evidence is unavailable
- Never guess missing information
- Never provide legal conclusions

If information is missing, the AI should return:

"Unable to Assess"

or

"Additional Information Required"

instead of guessing.

3. EVIDENCE-BASED FINDINGS

Verify every issue requires:

- Observed Fact
- Supporting Evidence
- Reasoning
- Compliance Concern
- Recommendation

The AI should separate:

WHAT IT DETECTED

from

WHAT IT BELIEVES THE RISK MAY BE

4. CONFIDENCE SCORING

Verify that all findings include confidence scoring.

Recommended ranges:

90-100 = Strong Evidence

75-89 = Likely Concern

50-74 = Potential Review Item

Below 50 = Insufficient Evidence

Findings below 75 confidence should not be classified as violations.

5. REGULATORY INTELLIGENCE

Verify the AI can assess:

- FDA Labeling
- FDA Allergen Requirements
- FTC Marketing Claims
- FTC Green Guides
- USDA Organic Claims
- Retail Packaging Requirements
- Consumer Safety Warnings

The AI should identify the regulatory framework being referenced.

6. CLAIM ANALYSIS

Verify the prompt teaches the AI how to evaluate:

- Natural
- Organic
- Healthy
- Sustainable
- Eco-Friendly
- Recyclable
- Compostable
- Non-GMO
- Gluten-Free
- Sugar-Free

The AI should flag substantiation risks when appropriate.

7. UNCERTAINTY MANAGEMENT

Verify the AI explains uncertainty.

Example:

Known Information:
...

Unknown Information:
...

Additional Information Needed:
...

Do not allow the AI to convert uncertainty into certainty.

8. HIGH-RISK FINDINGS

Verify that high-risk findings require:

- Strong supporting evidence
- High confidence
- Human review recommendation

If confidence is low, the AI should require human review.

9. OUTPUT QUALITY

Verify outputs include:

- Risk Score
- Confidence Score
- Severity
- Category
- Explanation
- Evidence
- Recommendation
- Human Review Indicator

10. LEGAL RISK CONTROL

Verify the prompt prevents:

"This package violates FDA regulations."

and prefers:

"This may present a potential FDA labeling concern and should be reviewed."

11. FINAL DISCLAIMER

Verify the AI clearly states:

This review is an AI-assisted compliance assessment and should not be considered legal advice, regulatory approval, or a definitive compliance determination.

FINAL DELIVERABLE

Provide:

- Overall Prompt Score /100
- Hallucination Protection Score /100
- Compliance Coverage Score /100
- Legal Risk Protection Score /100
- Enterprise Readiness Score /100

Then provide:

1. Findings
2. Risks
3. Missing Instructions
4. Recommended Additions

Only recommend additions that improve reliability and compliance accuracy.

Do not modify the implementation automatically.
Do not overwrite the existing prompt.
Do not remove existing functionality.
