BUILD AN ENTERPRISE-GRADE AI APPLICATION CALLED:

PACKAGING COMPLIANCE AI

MISSION

Build a Packaging Compliance Intelligence Platform that allows retailers, manufacturers, suppliers, packaging engineers, regulatory reviewers, legal teams, and compliance teams to upload packaging artwork and automatically identify compliance, regulatory, spelling, contextual language, and packaging defects before products go to production.

This is NOT a document management system.

This is NOT an approval workflow tool.

This is NOT a design tool.

This is an AI-powered Packaging Compliance Operating System.

The primary use case is supporting thousands of product packaging reviews across categories including food, beverage, household cleaners, chemicals, seasonal items, health & beauty, toys, pet products, and private-label retail products.

The system should be capable of processing individual reviews and large-scale batch reviews involving hundreds or thousands of SKUs.

====================================================
CORE BUSINESS PROBLEM
====================================================

Today packaging compliance reviews are manual.

Teams manually review:

- FDA requirements
- EPA requirements
- CPSC regulations
- Packaging requirements
- Internal SOPs
- Spelling errors
- Contextual language errors
- Missing disclosures
- Artwork issues

The platform should automate this review process.

Users should know within seconds:

1. Is the package compliant?
2. What failed?
3. Why did it fail?
4. What regulation was violated?
5. How can it be fixed?
6. Is production approval recommended?
