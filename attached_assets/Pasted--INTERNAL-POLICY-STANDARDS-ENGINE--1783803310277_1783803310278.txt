====================================================
INTERNAL POLICY & STANDARDS ENGINE
====================================================

Build an Internal Policy Management System.

This system will allow compliance managers and administrators to upload company-specific standards that influence compliance reviews.

The policies become part of the compliance engine and are validated alongside FDA, EPA, eCFR, and other regulatory sources.

====================================================
PURPOSE
====================================================

The platform must support both:

External Regulations

Examples:

- FDA
- EPA
- eCFR
- FTC
- USDA
- CPSC

Internal Standards

Examples:

- Dollar Tree Packaging Standards
- Brand Guidelines
- Supplier Standards
- Artwork Standards
- Compliance Procedures
- Legal Requirements
- Marketing Restrictions

Internal standards can generate violations even when no government regulation exists.

====================================================
SUCCESS CRITERIA
====================================================

The compliance engine must evaluate both regulatory requirements and internal company standards.

Internal policies should have equal authority to FDA and eCFR requirements during package reviews.

Users should be able to upload company documentation and immediately influence future compliance reviews without requiring software changes.
