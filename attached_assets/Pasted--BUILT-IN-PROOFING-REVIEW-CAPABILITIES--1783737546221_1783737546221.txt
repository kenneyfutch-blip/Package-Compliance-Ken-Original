====================================================
BUILT-IN PROOFING & REVIEW CAPABILITIES
====================================================

The application should include enterprise-grade proofing capabilities similar to Adobe Workfront, but enhanced with AI-powered compliance intelligence.

The goal is to provide a complete review experience where users can visually inspect packaging, collaborate with stakeholders, track revisions, and receive AI-generated compliance findings.

====================================================

====================================================

Support:

PDF

AI

Allow users to:

- View artwork directly in browser
- Zoom

- Rotate
- Review multipage packaging files
- View packaging at full resolution
- Navigate between versions

====================================================

Allow users to place comments directly on packaging.

Markup tools:

- Comment Pin
- Highlight

- Arrow
- Strike-through
- Text Note

Back panel ingredient section

All comments should support:

- Threaded replies
- Mentions (@user)
- Attachments
- Resolution status
- Priority level

====================================================

----------------------------------------------------

Retail marketing context.

----------------------------------------------------

Their / There / They're

Review contextual accuracy.

Provide explanation.
