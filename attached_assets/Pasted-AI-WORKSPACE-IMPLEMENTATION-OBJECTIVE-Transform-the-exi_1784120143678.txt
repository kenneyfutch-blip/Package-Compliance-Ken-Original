AI WORKSPACE IMPLEMENTATION

OBJECTIVE

Transform the existing AI Assistant into a full AI-native workspace that becomes the primary interface for interacting with Packaging Assets, Compliance Reviews, Regulatory Intelligence, Reports, Tasks, Specialists, and future AI Agents.

IMPORTANT

Do NOT replace the existing AI Assistant.

Do NOT remove any current functionality.

Enhance the existing assistant and create an advanced AI Workspace experience accessible through the current AI Assistant interface.

The AI Workspace will eventually serve as the operating layer for future Claude-powered agents while integrating with the existing OpenAI-powered Compliance Intelligence platform.
